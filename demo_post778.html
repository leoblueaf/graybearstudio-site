Minutes

Record the meeting on a phone and get a first draft of the minutes, or upload minutes the adjutant already wrote. Nothing is official until the post approves it at the next meeting.

  1. Announce at the start that the meeting is being recorded for the minutes.
  2. Press Record and set the phone on the table.
  3. After the meeting, a draft is written in the post's order of business.
  4. The adjutant fixes names, motions and votes.
  5. The post approves the minutes; the recording is deleted.
