**From the device you used.** Open the quilt for the day you added your square, tap **Mine** (or find your square), and choose **Remove my square**. It disappears from the quilt, the archive and search at once.

**From any other device, or if you cleared your browser.** Email [privacy@graybearstudio.com](mailto:privacy@graybearstudio.com) with the square code. The code is the short set of six letters and numbers shown on the square's card, for example `K7Q2RM`. If you don't have the code, tell us the date and the words of the square. We'll remove it from the quilt, the archive and search, usually within 7 days.

**Someone else's square about you.** Email the same address with the square code or the date and words, and we'll take a look.
