Last updated: October 28, 2026

The Hitherto Prayer Quilt is a public website made by GrayBear Studio. It is separate from the Hitherto app. The app keeps your prayers on your iPhone and never sends anything to the quilt. This notice covers the quilt website only.

**Squares are public.** Anything you add to the quilt can be read, searched and shared by anyone, now and in the archive of past quilts. Please use first names or initials only.

**What we store for each square:** the prayer text, the initials you choose to add, the fabric you pick, a short square code, and the date and time it was added. We also store how many knots and reports each square has, and a count of squares added through a church's referral link.

**A random device ID.** When you add a square, tie a knot or report a square, our provider gives your browser a random ID through anonymous sign-in. We keep it privately next to your square or knot so that each device can add one square a day and tie one knot per square, and so you can remove your own square. It is never shown on the quilt and is not linked to your name or email.

**On your device.** Your browser keeps the codes of squares you added and the knots you tied, so the quilt can show "Your square" and "You tied a knot". Clearing your browser data removes them.

**Our providers.** The quilt runs on Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication, in the United States). Google reCAPTCHA helps keep automated spam out. These services process technical information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the site, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We do not store IP addresses ourselves. Google's published terms for these services commit it to protecting this information at least as strongly as this notice does, so it receives the same or equal protection; if that ever stops being true, we will stop using the service and update this notice.

**What we don't do.** No accounts for visitors. No advertising, no analytics and no tracking across sites. We do not sell or share information for advertising.

**Review.** Some squares are checked before they appear, and reported squares may be hidden while they are reviewed. Squares that mention self-harm or suicide are not posted or stored; you'll see where to find help instead.

**How long squares stay.** Squares remain part of that day's quilt until they are removed, or until we close the quilt. Held squares that aren't approved are deleted.

**Removing a square.** On the device you used, open your square and tap Remove my square. Or email [privacy@graybearstudio.com](mailto:privacy@graybearstudio.com) with the square code, and we'll remove it from the quilt, the archive and search, usually within 7 days.

**Your rights.** You can ask us about, or ask us to delete, information connected with a square you added. Email [privacy@graybearstudio.com](mailto:privacy@graybearstudio.com).

**Age.** The quilt is for people 13 and older. Please don't share details about children.

**Changes.** If this notice changes, we will update this page and the date above.

**Contact.** [privacy@graybearstudio.com](mailto:privacy@graybearstudio.com)
