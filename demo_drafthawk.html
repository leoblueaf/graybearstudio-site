Play this on a laptop, tablet or second phone on the outside of the closed door, a foot or two from it. Use the same frequency on both devices. High tones (16 to 18 kHz) may be quiet or inaudible to you; that is normal. If the iPhone shows no peak at 16k, the speaker may not reach it: try 12k.
Test plan (back door)
  1. Second device outside the door: Tone tab, 16000 Hz, Play. Close the door.
  2. iPhone inside: Listen tab, Tone mode, 16000, Start mic. Allow the microphone.
  3. Stand in the middle of the room, tap Zero, hold still 2 s.
  4. Hold the bottom edge of the iPhone about 1 inch from the door. Record 3 s at: the latch-side gap, the hinge side, the bottom sweep, and the solid middle of the door panel.
  5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 at 12000 and 4000 Hz.
  6. Optional: Hiss mode with no tone, kitchen hood and bath fans on, same spots.
  7. Copy results and paste them into the chat.
Pass: the gap reads at least 10 dB above the solid door at one frequency, and you can find the gap by sweeping without knowing where it is.
