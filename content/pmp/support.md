### Do I need an account?
Yes. PMP Reasoning keeps your progress, flags, notes and streak in your account so they follow you between your iPhone and iPad. Sign-up takes an email address and a display name.

### How much can I try before paying?
Ten questions, across any study path. The Mindset Rules, the Mindset Guide and the Glossary stay free for everyone with no limit.

### How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Open the subscription screen and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Your study progress comes back when you sign in to your PMP Reasoning account.

### How do I cancel?
On your device, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > PMP Reasoning, then cancel. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

### I have a redemption code. How do I use it?
Tap the code's link on the device you want it on, sign in to the App Store if asked, and confirm. Install PMP Reasoning from the App Store, then create or sign in to your account. Premium unlocks on its own. If it does not, tap Restore Purchase.

### How do I delete my account and everything in it?
Open Account, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete Account. It is immediate and permanent, and there is no undo. Cancel any Apple subscription separately.

### Are these real exam questions?
No. These are original questions written to the exam's published format and content outline. The app does not promise a passing score.

### The chart on a question did not appear.
Close and reopen the question. If it is still missing, email us the question's topic and your iPhone or iPad model. A chart that fails to draw shows as empty space rather than an error, so a report is the only way we see it.

### Does it work on iPad?
Yes. The same account and the same subscription cover both.
