### How do I make my own glass?
Tap the photo button, choose a photo, then trace around the part you want with your finger or use the whole photo. It turns into glass right away.

### What do the tools do?
Slice cuts along your finger. Crack adds cracks that never come apart. Move lets you drag the glass and flick it. Float makes the pieces drift slowly. The reset button puts everything back together.

### How do I keep the cracks without the glass breaking?
Choose Crack, then tap, hold or swipe on the glass. It keeps every crack and stays in one piece. Tap Freeze when it looks right.

### The menus disappeared. How do I get them back?
They hide while you play so nothing is in the way. Tap the small glowing bear in the corner to bring them back.

### What are the hidden tricks in the light toys?
Hold your finger still for a few seconds, or use two fingers and pinch or spread. Each toy does something different.

### How do I save a picture?
Tap Freeze, then Save to Photos. The first time, your iPhone asks for permission to add photos.

### I don't feel any vibrations.
Check that System Haptics is on in Settings, then Sounds & Haptics.

### I don't hear any sound.
Turn up the volume and check that your iPhone is not in Silent Mode.

### The animation looks slower than usual.
Low Power Mode limits how smoothly iPhones draw animations. Turn it off in Settings, then Battery, for the smoothest motion.

### Does Glass it up need the internet?
No. Everything happens on your iPhone, and nothing is uploaded.

### What does the Full Unlock include?
All eleven light toys, Jelly glass, the Bloom tool, 3D glass and 4K saves, for one payment. No subscription. You can try any locked toy for 20 seconds first.

### I bought the Full Unlock. How do I get it back on a new iPhone?
Open Settings in the app (the sliders button), tap Unlock, then Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
